# UC-11 Manage Cart

**Primary actor:** Buyer

**Goal:** Manage the buyer's provisional product selection.

**Preconditions**
- The buyer exists and is authorized.
- The selected product is available for selection.

**Main flow**
1. The buyer adds, removes, or clears products.
2. The application validates the operation.
3. The cart is updated.
4. The updated cart is returned.

**Postconditions**
- The buyer's cart reflects the requested operation.
